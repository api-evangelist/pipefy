An IT modernization strategy is the plan for upgrading enterprise technology to meet new demands without breaking what already works. For legacy-heavy enterprises, the smartest path is rarely a full replacement. It is modernizing in layers and orchestrating people, systems, and AI Agents on top of the stack you already run.
